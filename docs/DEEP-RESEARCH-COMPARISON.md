# Deep research: patterns to adopt, evidence to earn

Reviewed 2026-10-19. Operator-led primary-source research and selected local source inspection; this comparison was **not generated by a TMF Council run**. Runtime baseline: TMF Research `d20ccdc`. The changes below are proposals, not implemented features.

## Recommendation

Improve evidence acquisition and question closure before expanding the cast of agents. Adopt editable plans and source controls from ChatGPT's documented experience; a richer search/read/research loop from Perplexity's documented interfaces; and evaluate Crawlee as an optional collection worker. Preserve our REA evidence, explicit uncertainty, proposed requirements and editable exports.

This is an architectural recommendation based on public material and our current source. It is not proof of either vendor's private implementation or a measured performance comparison.

## What our current implementation actually does

[engine.py](../tmf_research/engine.py) has a lead, source scout, three bounded specialists, claim review, Council perspectives, follow-up, synthesis and a final support review. It retains fetched evidence locally. [providers.py](../tmf_research/providers.py) pins validated public HTTPS addresses for the existing fetch transport. Those are useful foundations.

The examined customer-service run used Detailed mode, 17 role calls, eight web-source attempts plus selected REA evidence. It retained 12 supported and four proposed findings, 14 requirements and three withheld uncertain claims. Four of its nine source records were partial. See the [published example and limits](../examples/council-live/README.md).

Current limits materially affect depth:

- Analyst packets contain only the first 16,000 characters of extracted page text. A retained full snapshot does not mean a specialist read its relevant later section.
- Deep mode caps web-source attempts at 12 and follow-up rounds at two. These are ceilings, not completeness criteria.
- Follow-up requests are collected, but the pipeline lacks a durable per-question closure record linking the original objection to resolving evidence.
- Accepted findings accumulate across roles and rounds. Repeated supported claims can inflate apparent breadth; the sample has visible overlap.
- Plain HTML extraction has no demonstrated rendered-page/PDF acquisition path in this engine.
- Model review is useful filtering, not independent factual certification. Commercial claims still lack buyer interviews, measured work and real quotes.

## Proposed changes, in implementation order

| Priority | Change | Concrete behavior | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P0 | Question-linked passage retrieval | Split retained text into stable, overlapping passages with source hash and offsets. Retrieve relevant passages for each research question; expand neighbors before reviewing support. Record which passages each role received. | A frozen fixture places decisive evidence after character 16,000. The new path retrieves it and answers correctly; the prefix-only baseline misses it. A nearby qualification must survive expansion. |
| P0 | Question and gap ledger | Give every material question an ID, priority, evidence need, attempts and status: open, supported answer, conflicting evidence, unavailable, budget exhausted or requires external validation. Closure needs linked evidence and review. | Repeated objections merge into one question. An unsuccessful search stays open; a budget stop stays budget exhausted. A proposed test never counts as execution evidence. |
| P0 | Adaptive follow-up within budgets | Let the lead choose the next search, page read or targeted inspection from unresolved questions. Stop on explicit coverage, exhausted budget, unavailable evidence or diminishing useful results. Reserve capacity for review/export. | A documented stop reason and final ledger account for every critical question. A fixture contradiction triggers a targeted search instead of another generic specialist summary. |
| P0 | Consolidate findings without losing provenance | Produce one presented finding per distinct supported proposition, retaining contributing claim IDs, passages, caveats and dissent. Review consolidated wording again. | Duplicate claims collapse; different scope, dates or qualifications do not disappear. Consolidation cannot upgrade confidence or combine partial evidence into invented certainty. |
| P1 | Editable research plan | Present specific questions and intended evidence alongside existing selectable lenses. Ask only for missing input that changes the decision; make sensible defaults easy to accept. | Editing a question changes the executed plan and saved revision. Intake does not force unnecessary confirmation rounds. |
| P1 | Enforced source policy | Separate preferred sites from allowed sites; add time/jurisdiction scope. Apply restrictions during discovery and again at fetch/redirect boundaries. | A restricted run cannot quietly admit a forbidden redirect. Unavailable official evidence remains unavailable; a secondary source is explicitly labeled. |
| P1 | Source-to-passage citation inspection | Clicking a claim should show its retained passage and qualification, snapshot date, hash and source URL. Distinguish searched, fetched, read, cited and inspected. | Every factual claim resolves to the exact retained evidence it used. Broken, irrelevant and misleading citations fail review. |
| P1 | Targeted rendered collection | Add an optional worker for pages that truly require rendering, with a typed evidence return contract and resource/egress limits. | Static and rendered fixtures preserve identity and full relevant text. Denied, blocked and failed pages do not become successful empty records. |
| P1 | Measured quality and budget reporting | Score against decision-specific questions and critical facts; report elapsed time, calls, available token usage and unresolved items. Unknown monetary cost remains unknown. | More agents, citations, words or sources cannot alone increase the quality score. |
| P2 | Optional model diversity and numerical analysis | Permit separately configured review providers; isolate reproducible calculations when needed. | Provider identity and disagreements are retained. Agreements are not proof. Calculation inputs, units, code and outputs are inspectable; no arbitrary source-issued execution. |

These are original TMF requirements inspired by documented patterns, not claims that either competitor implements this exact ledger or acceptance contract.

## Crawlee: useful collection infrastructure

[Crawlee](https://github.com/apify/crawlee) documents HTTP and browser crawling, URL queues, pluggable storage, resource scaling, lifecycle hooks and retries. [BasicCrawler options](https://crawlee.dev/js/api/basic-crawler/interface/BasicCrawlerOptions) expose crawl/retry/concurrency controls and optional robots handling. Its [license](https://github.com/apify/crawlee/blob/02899241d570624a0ce0b77dbcb44b9d58a98ade/LICENSE.md) is Apache-2.0. These are collection capabilities; they do not decide whether a claim is supported.

### Actual source inspection

Privately cloned the default branch at `02899241d570624a0ce0b77dbcb44b9d58a98ade`. Its package manifest declares 4.0.0 and Node >=22.13.0; GitHub's [latest stable release](https://github.com/apify/crawlee/releases/tag/v3.18.2) was 3.18.2 at review time. Pin and test a chosen release; do not assume current master and stable documentation are interchangeable.

REA inspected exactly `packages/basic-crawler/src/internals/basic-crawler.ts` and `packages/playwright-crawler/src/internals/playwright-crawler.ts` as inert source. It parsed two files with zero parse failures. Parent evidence: `ev_5469c62f86cf966680aec2df64709b5e32631534485f8018a04989f4cf29f067`; artifact identity: `6cb3e30664f9c08b08db6d8a719504e96c56481ab400719260022df539dd3105`. The semantic graph hit configured node limits and is partial; 1,806 inventoried relevant files were unselected. This is **not runtime, complete-architecture or security assurance**.

Direct reading of [the pinned BasicCrawler source](https://github.com/apify/crawlee/blob/02899241d570624a0ce0b77dbcb44b9d58a98ade/packages/basic-crawler/src/internals/basic-crawler.ts) found robots handling defaulted off, optional page/depth limits, default retry count three and no same-domain delay by default. Our worker would set policy and budgets explicitly. Browser dependencies are separate from Crawlee in the README's setup example.

### Proposed adapter boundary

Keep Python as the research coordinator. Use a separately bounded Node worker through the existing argument-array process seam when a configured acquisition strategy selects Crawlee. Plain public HTML can continue through the current lightweight fetcher. The worker should return normalized source records, not final research conclusions.

Each result needs requested/final URL, redirects, status, timestamp, content type, raw/derived artifact hashes, extraction method, retained text and its locations, truncation, retry history and failure reason. Preserve the raw page/rendered evidence separately from derived text. Isolate storage by run; a saved queue is not evidence of a completed document or a resumable model investigation.

Collection acceptance must cover:

- Explicit approved scope, robots policy, concurrency, retry, byte/page/time and storage limits; enforce hard reservations where parallel work could exceed a soft library limit.
- Public-only egress for documents, redirects, robots lookups and browser subresources. Preserve the existing fetcher's public-address protections; do not assume a browser worker inherits them.
- Isolated browser state with no inherited personal sessions; failed pages and exhausted retries remain explicit.
- Cancellation cleans up only owned workers; restart reconciles queue entries and saved artifacts before claiming recovery.
- Content-based duplicate detection preserves distinct URLs and retrieval history. No automatic authenticated crawling or block-bypass requirement in the initial candidate.

No dependencies were installed, no browser crawl was run, and no runtime adapter was added in this investigation. Crawlee is a strong candidate for a controlled collection trial, not an already integrated capability.

## How we judge the improvement

Freeze the customer-service question, evidence snapshots, critical facts and allowed budget before a before/after comparison. Add separate public tasks covering a QuickBooks-style workflow feature map, an ecommerce returns process and a jurisdiction-specific payroll intake checklist. Prepare an original task rubric before viewing outputs.

Score supported factual accuracy, question coverage, contradiction handling, useful requirements, duplicate claims, citation support and honest uncertainty. Track cost/time separately. Unsupported guarantees, fabricated prices and claimed-but-unperformed runtime tests are critical failures. A model judge can assist; inspect critical claims directly against sources.

Use two tests: fixed snapshots isolate retrieval/reasoning changes; a separately dated live-web test measures current acquisition. Do not conflate them. Any head-to-head product run needs the same brief, available sources and disclosed budget/settings; none was performed here. Repeat before claiming a reliable gain. A vendor benchmark is a useful methodology reference, not our result.

## Immediate choice

Start with passage retrieval, a question ledger and evidence-preserving consolidation. Then trial Crawlee on one public static site and one rendered fixture through the acquisition boundary. Editable planning and broader provider integrations follow demonstrated research-quality gains. The intended product is a defensible answer and usable build/service specification, not an impressive count of agents or documents.
