# TMF intelligence and production bundle

A durable capability map for the system Andy calls the MC “supercomputer.” Research is one capability; the larger job is to collect, understand, produce, organize and supply useful work to the rest of MC. This is a descriptive bundle title, not a product rename or a claim that everything is implemented.

As of 2026-10-20. [Machine-readable bundle](intelligence-production-bundle.json). Canon remains the current decision/work record. This document consolidates scope and acceptance; it does not replace Canon, the existing roadmap, catalog owners or the builder backlog.

## What must remain in scope

One connected system can support research, design, software, operations, clients and revenue. A job can be a regular scrape followed by one specialist and a report; it does not need a deep Council session by default. Quick selectable modes should cover job, depth, lenses, audience and multiple output formats. Deep investigation and Council are available where warranted.

The broad sequence is:

**Request or authorized trigger → select context/sources → collect → inspect/normalize → appropriate specialist → optional Council → produce → review → hand off to the existing owner → verify use and retain outcomes.**

Use the original site's existing categories when available. ## Job and automation contract

The machine bundle contains a proposed common job envelope: stable ID/version, measurable outcome, job/lens/depth selection, relevant specialist/Council, source and client scope, context/pack versions, budgets, actor authority, outputs, lifecycle, review state, acceptance and refresh rules.

Planned triggers include manual requests, scheduled refreshes, new sources, incoming builder tickets/builds and maintenance downtime checks. Simple jobs can use an existing light workflow; complex builder tasks may need an existing node-workflow plugin. Discover and evaluate what is already installed before building an orchestration engine.

Every recurring job needs bounded run/cost/page/byte/model budgets, stop/cancel behavior, retained failures, duplicate protection, recovery ownership and change-only notifications. A failed read is not a zero result. Do not silently retry consequential external effects. Researching prospects does not authorize sending outreach; information collection does not authorize trades, payments or filings.

## Models and specialist routing

Use the inexpensive workable configuration first. Retain the recommended richer setup as an optional configuration, subject to real evaluation. Jev/Kev/Tev/Maya/GPT/Perplexity-style routing or decision candidates can help choose work, depth, decomposition, role and output; their availability and performance must be checked rather than assumed. Do not replace substantive investigation or evidence review with a confidence label.

Current research provider behavior is documented in the roadmap; no provider change, per-job price, premium fallback or benchmark win is established by this bundle. Select only the specialists needed and reuse retained evidence. Larger role counts are not a product-quality claim.

## Outputs and standalone use

Deliver actual editable/downloadable files: Markdown/HTML, PDF, Word, spreadsheets/CSV, presentations, diagrams, knowledge chunks and machine-readable builder contracts. Match the document's structure to the job and audience; do not simply pour the same text into every format. WordCraft/GridCraft/DeckCraft and diagram/design adapters are existing candidates/engines with their own verification boundaries.

Keep the MIT standalone project useful outside MC with installation/configuration, examples, bounded jobs and real exports. Connected MC use can add scoped context, specialist knowledge, plugin/tool access, persistent canvas work and operational handoff. The public tool and demonstrable services can support TMF marketing on X/LinkedIn; they do not yet prove market demand, stars or revenue. A saleable design/knowledge production service must demonstrate a repeatable useful result.
